The insight · two layers

Coordinated silence is a sensor.

Layer 1 — detection is aggregate

A grid of sentinel devices

  • municipal or operator-owned SIMs, one per cell — never the public
  • already deployed: meters, traffic lights, pumps, site monitors — opted in by their owners
  • read every 30 s through Congestion Insights and Device Reachability
  • the stable CAMARA endpoints are device-scoped, so a fixed SIM per cell is how an area-level read is built
Layer 2 — triage is consented

An opt-in registry

  • elderly, disabled, pilgrimage groups, lone workers — people who chose to be findable
  • queried only inside a declared footprint, and only for those who opted in
  • who is unreachable, and where they were last seen
detection touches no member of the public, everthe register is held by the agency; operators honour it

Two gates — can veto, never cause
  • contiguous silence — a connected block at or above the size floor
  • local baseline — the silence must be abnormal here, against these cells' own history

One engine · many hazards

The network already knows.
Nabd makes it say so — in the first minute.

Impact

Earthquake, flood, storm, mass outage — to the network each is an area going dark. The first dispatch decision moves from hours to minutes.

Who buys it · what it costs

Civil-defence agencies, Red Crescent societies, municipalities — through the operator, as an Open Gateway impact feed. 7,836 sentinel SIMs watch all of Türkiye: the bill scales with land area, not population.

Roadmap

Geofencing for boundary events · QoD priority for responders inside the footprint · an aggregate network surface the day one ships.
